# The GM Guide

**Audience:** GMs running a game with Blacksmith.

A session in the order it runs: what to set up once, what to do as the players arrive, during
exploration, in a fight, and at the end of the night. Each section points at the guide that says
more.

## Set up the campaign, once

Under **Campaign Settings**, give the campaign its **Campaign Name** and choose the **Rules
Version**, 2024 or 2014, which changes how rules such as exhaustion are handled. **Number of
Rulebooks** and the compendium drop-downs it exposes say which rulebooks the campaign draws on,
and **Custom Rulebooks** names any that are not compendiums. Under **Party**, set the **Party
Name**, the **Party Size** and the character drop-downs it exposes, and the **Default Party
Makeup** and **Default Party Level** that new narratives assume. Under **Geography**, the
**Realm**, **Region**, **Site**, and **Area** seed every scene that has not been told otherwise;
see [the scenes guide](userguide-scenes.md).

Then walk **Imports** to point **Compendium Mapping** at the compendiums you use, and **Notes** to
choose the journal that holds everyone's notes. Nothing else has to be set before the first
session; change what gets in the way as you go.

## As the players arrive

- **Set the session timer** from the bar so everyone can see how long is left; see
  [the timers guide](userguide-timers.md).
- **Choose a party leader** from the crown, or run a leader vote; see
  [the Blacksmith bar guide](userguide-menubar.md) and [the votes guide](userguide-votes.md).
- **Check the clock** on the bar and set the time and date if the story has moved on; see
  [the world clock guide](userguide-worldclock.md).
- **Send a toast** to welcome the table or remind them of something, from the bullhorn.

## Exploring

- **Set the movement mode** from the bar: **Wander** for a town, **Conga** or **Fastest Path** to
  march behind the leader, **Locked** while you narrate, **Request** when every step matters.
- **Let time pass** with the clock's arrows or a running time mode, and let the scene's light
  follow it.
- **Ask for a roll** from the dice button: a skill for one character, a group check for the whole
  party, a contest between two sides, or a cinematic that fills every screen; see
  [the rolls guide](userguide-rolls.md).
- **Keep the map annotated** with pins from journal pages and notes, and hide the ones the players
  should not see yet; see [the pins guide](userguide-pins.md).
- **Keep your own notes** in the Notes window and on the sheets of items and actors as GM notes;
  see [the notes guide](userguide-notes.md).
- **Run an encounter from its journal page** when the fight arrives: deploy the monsters from the
  toolbar at the top of the page; see [the journals guide](userguide-journals.md).

## In a fight

- **Start the combat** with **Create** on the bar, roll initiative from the combat bar, and run the
  turns from it. Drag portraits to reorder initiative. Dead NPCs are marked defeated for you; see
  [the combat guide](userguide-combat.md).
- **Watch the timers**: the planning timer before each round's first turn, the turn timer under
  the current combatant, and the **Hurry Up** nudge for a slow player.
- **Read the readout** above the portraits for damage, accuracy, kills, and the leading MVP, and
  the encounter difficulty as the sides change.
- **Keep an eye on the canvas**: rings mark the current turn and targets, and blood pools grow
  under the wounded. Clear it all with **Remove All Blood** when the fight is over; see
  [the tokens guide](userguide-tokens.md).

## When the fight ends

- **Award experience** from the XP Distribution window that opens on its own, checking each
  adversary's resolution and adding a milestone if the story earned one; see
  [the experience guide](userguide-xp.md).
- **Share the summary**: the round and combat cards, the MVP, and the notable moments go to the
  players if you have chosen to share them; see [the statistics guide](userguide-statistics.md).

## Resting

Open **Rest and Recovery** from the clock menu, tick who is resting, choose short or long, and press
**Begin Rest**. Each character gets a card to rest from, the clock moves once when the last of them
has rested, and a long rest can track food and water; see
[the world clock guide](userguide-worldclock.md).

## Ending the night

- **Run an End Time vote** when the table is flagging; see [the votes guide](userguide-votes.md).
- **Check the records** in Party Statistics, and export them before clearing anything.
- **Add the next session's events** to the World Calendar, and set a reminder on any note you want
  back at the start of the next session.

## Bringing content in

Journals, items, actors, and roll tables can be imported from JSON, written by hand from a template
or generated by an outside tool from a prompt the Import window writes for you. Validate first,
then import; a re-import updates rather than duplicates; and an imported page lands in the world,
not in a compendium. See [the importing guide](userguide-import.md).

## Deciding what the players see

Most of Blacksmith's settings are yours and apply to the whole world. The ones that decide what
players are shown are worth knowing by name:

| What | Setting |
|---|---|
| Whether players get the round and combat statistics cards | **Share With Players** under **Statistics** |
| Whether players see the XP card | **Share Results** under **XP Distribution** |
| Whether players see enemy health in the tracker and on the bar | **Hide Enemy Health Bars**, in both places |
| Whether players see blood on the canvas | **Token Blood Visibility** under **Health Indicators** |
| Whether players see the planning and turn timers | **GM-Only Timers** under **Timers** |
| Whether players may drag from Compendium Search onto their sheets | **Players Can Add From Compendium Search** |
| Whether players may create pins | **Player Pin Editing** under **Pins** |
| Whether players may use the manual rolls button | **Player Manual Rolls** under **Sidebars** |
| How each kind of announcement reaches the table | The channel choices under **Notifications**: a toast, a chat card, both, or neither, per feature |
| Who never sees a toast | **Excluded Users** under **Notifications** and **Toasts**, for a stream or camera account |
