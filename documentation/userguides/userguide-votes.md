# Votes

**Audience:** GMs and party leaders putting a question to the table, and players answering it.

How to start a vote, what each built-in vote asks, how a player casts and changes their vote, and
how a vote closes and settles a tie.

## Start a vote

The GM, or the party leader. Press **Start Vote** in the Blacksmith group of Foundry's scene
controls, or click the crown on the Blacksmith bar and choose **Start a Vote**. Only one vote runs
at a time; starting another while one is open is refused. The **Start a Vote** window offers:

| Vote | Who can start it | What it asks |
|---|---|---|
| **Select a Leader** | GM only | Vote for a party leader from among the active players. Each online player's character is an option. |
| **Yes or No** | GM or leader | A simple yes or no for a quick decision. |
| **End Time** | GM or leader | When to end the session: **Stop now**, **End of this round**, **End of combat**, **30 more minutes**, or **Only if I pass out**. |
| **Party Plan** | GM or leader | How to approach the situation: **I want to hit stuff**, **Let's talk to them**, **I prefer to avoid this**, or **I can roll with whatever**. |
| **Character Vote** | GM or leader | A vote between characters drawn from a source you choose: **Current Players**, **Heroes in the Party**, **Heroes on the Canvas**, **Monster Combatants**, **Selected Tokens**, or **Targeted Tokens**. |
| **Custom Vote** | GM or leader | Your own question. **Enter vote title**, then the options, one per line; at least two are needed. |

A vote needs somebody to cast it: eligible voters are the players who are logged in and own a
character. With none online the vote is refused and says why.

## Cast a vote

Any logged-in player who owns a character. The vote arrives as a chat card with a button for each
option. Click one to vote; the card shows your choice under **Your vote**, and you can click a
different option to change it while the vote is open. The GM does not vote. The card also shows how
the vote is going, and the GM and the leader can see **Who voted for what**.

## Close a vote

The person who started it, or on its own. The vote closes by itself the moment every eligible
voter has cast a vote. Before that, whoever started it can close it from the card. The card then
shows the result.

A **Select a Leader** vote makes the winner the party leader straight away, with the same
announcement as choosing one from the bar. If two or more characters tie for leader, the GM gets a
**Leader Vote Tie** dialog and picks between them with **Choose Leader**. Other votes simply report
the tie.
