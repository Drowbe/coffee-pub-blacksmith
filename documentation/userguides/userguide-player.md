# The Player Guide

**Audience:** players in a game that uses Blacksmith.

What you see, what you can do, and what only the GM can do, in the order you will meet it. Each
section points at the guide that says more.

## The bar across the top

You see the Blacksmith bar with its tools on the left and, on the right, the world clock, the party
leader, the movement mode, and the session timer. The right-hand readouts are yours to read, not to
change: only the GM sets the leader, the movement mode, and the timer, and only the GM moves the
clock. [The Blacksmith bar guide](userguide-menubar.md) covers every tool.

You can:

- **Roll your own dice** from the dice button, which opens the Dice Tray for you, and turn manual
  dice entry on for yourself from its right-click menu.
- **See the party's health** in the Health window. The GM's controls for changing hit points are
  not shown to you.
- **Apply and clear conditions** on your own character from the Status Effects window. Select your
  token first; a token you do not own can be looked at but not changed.
- **Keep your own macros** in the Macros window and your favourites in its right-click menu.
- **Write notes** and decide who reads them; see [the notes guide](userguide-notes.md).
- **Search the compendiums** and, if the GM allows it, drag what you find onto your sheet.
- **Hide the interface** from the menu at the left of the bar, for a clean look at the map.

## If you are the party leader

The crown on the bar shows your character. You can start a vote from the crown or from the **Start
Vote** button in the scene controls, send any toast the GM has shared with you from the bullhorn's
right-click menu, and move freely when the party is in Conga or Fastest Path mode while the others
follow you. See [the votes guide](userguide-votes.md).

## Moving your token

What you can do with your token depends on the movement mode on the bar. In **Wander** you move
freely; in **Locked** you cannot move at all; in **Combat** you move only on your own turn; in
**Conga** and **Fastest Path** only the leader moves and you follow; and in **Request** each move
asks the GM's permission first. The bar tells you which mode is on, and a toast tells you when it
changes.

## When the GM asks for a roll

A card arrives in chat naming the roll and listing who is to make it. Click your character's row to
roll. The Roll Configuration window lets you add a bonus, pick advantage or disadvantage, and set
the roll's privacy; then the result goes onto the card for everyone entitled to see it. If the GM
made it a cinematic, the request fills your screen instead, and you click your own card there. See
[the rolls guide](userguide-rolls.md).

## During a fight

The combat bar under the top bar shows every combatant's portrait and the round and turn; the
readout above it shows the party's damage, accuracy, kills, and who is leading for MVP. You can
watch it but not drive it: the turn controls, dragging initiative, and the portrait menus are the
GM's. Foundry's combat tracker gains health bars and portraits, and **Roll Remaining** rolls your
own character's initiative when the round starts, if the GM has not done it for you.

In the tracker you also see the timers. On your turn, the turn timer carries an **END TURN**
overlay you can click; on someone else's turn it carries a hurry-up nudge instead. A planning timer
may run before each round's first turn. See [the combat guide](userguide-combat.md) and
[the timers guide](userguide-timers.md).

On the canvas you may see rings around the current combatant and around targeted tokens, and blood
under wounded creatures; the GM chooses whether players see the blood. See
[the tokens guide](userguide-tokens.md).

## After a fight

If the GM shares them, cards summarise the round and the combat, name an MVP, and list notable
moments. When experience is awarded, an XP card says what each character received and the number
is written to your sheet. See [the statistics guide](userguide-statistics.md) and
[the experience guide](userguide-xp.md).

## Resting

When the GM starts a rest, a card for your character appears in chat with one button: press it to
take the rest. On a short rest you may get buttons to spend hit dice one at a time; on a long rest
you may be asked to forage if you have no food or water. See
[the world clock guide](userguide-worldclock.md).

## Votes

A vote arrives as a card with a button per option. Click to vote, click another to change your mind
while it is open, and read the result when it closes. You can vote if you are logged in and own a
character. See [the votes guide](userguide-votes.md).

## Pins and the calendar

Pins on the map open what they point at with a left-click. You can place and edit pins only if the
GM has allowed player pin editing, and you can always choose which pins show on your own screen
from the pins button on the bar. The World Calendar, when the GM opens it for you, lets you add and
edit your own events. See [the pins guide](userguide-pins.md) and
[the world clock guide](userguide-worldclock.md).

## Your settings

Most of Blacksmith's settings are the GM's and are hidden from you. The ones you can change are
about your own screen: which tools sit on your bar, whether your sidebar is restyled or pinned open,
how your combat bar is sized and shaped, whether your combat tracker opens on its own and can be
resized, and whether your character's initiative is rolled for you. They are under **Coffee Pub
Blacksmith** in Foundry's **Configure Settings**, under the same headings the GM sees.
