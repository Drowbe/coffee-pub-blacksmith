# Testing: shared Tool window controls

**Audience:** us.

Scope: `.blacksmith-tool-btn`, `.blacksmith-tool-segmented` and the themed action bar buttons (`styles/window-tool-controls.css`), and the rest window as their first consumer (`scripts/window-rest.js`). Nothing here can be checked without looking at a window. Remove an item when it passes, and delete this file when it is empty. Results go to the **Verified** line of the CHANGELOG entry, not back into this file.

Open the rest window on each Tool theme: Light, Glass and dark. Switch themes from the window's menu, under Theme.

1. Segmented control look. Expected: one rounded bordered container holding Long Rest and Short Rest; the selected one is filled orange with white text; the other is transparent with muted text. Same on all three themes.
2. Segmented control behaviour. Click Short Rest. Expected: it fills orange, Long Rest empties, and the form below changes as before (hit dice show, provisions and hit points hide). Click Long Rest and it returns.
3. Keyboard. Tab to the segmented control, then press the Left and Right arrow keys. Expected: the selection moves between the two, the form follows, and a focus ring shows on the focused segment.
4. All and None. Expected: two small bordered buttons in the "Who is resting" caption, 24px high, readable on every theme. Clicking each ticks or clears the roster as before.
5. Footer. Expected: Cancel on the left is a quiet themed button and Begin Rest on the right is orange with white text; both readable on Light, and both still work. Hover each: Begin Rest brightens, Cancel gets the hover surface.
6. Disabled. If you can reach a state with a disabled shared button, expected: dimmed to about a third of its strength with no hover.
7. Standard windows are unchanged. Open an ordinary Blacksmith window that uses the action bar, such as Configure Pin. Expected: its Save and Cancel buttons look exactly as before.
8. The other button states. Add this markup to any Tool window body and check it on each theme: `<button class="blacksmith-tool-btn">Plain</button> <button class="blacksmith-tool-btn is-active">Active</button> <button class="blacksmith-tool-btn is-danger">Danger</button> <button class="blacksmith-tool-btn is-icon" data-tooltip="Icon"><i class="fa-solid fa-xmark"></i></button>`. Expected: 24px high, active in orange on a tint, danger turns red on hover, the icon button is square.
9. Foundry's own button styling. Expected: none of the buttons above is taller or darker than described, which would mean Foundry's own `button` rule outranks the shared one.
10. Section. In the rest window, expected: each group has an uppercase caption with its icon, a thin rule above every group after the first, and the All and None buttons sit at the right of the "Who is resting" caption. Readable on Light, Glass and dark. In any section with two rows of buttons and a list, expected: a small gap between each, nothing touching.
11. Hint and empty state. Expected: the grey note under Provisions, Hit Points and Hit Dice is one muted line, and with no player characters the italic "No player characters were found" shows. Readable on all three themes.
12. Card. Add this to a Tool window body: `<div class="blacksmith-tool-card"><div class="blacksmith-tool-card-head"><label class="blacksmith-tool-card-summary"><input type="checkbox" class="blacksmith-tool-card-toggle"><i class="blacksmith-tool-card-caret fa-solid fa-chevron-right"></i><span class="blacksmith-tool-card-title">A card</span><span class="blacksmith-tool-card-badges"><span class="blacksmith-badge">3</span></span></label><span class="blacksmith-tool-card-actions"><button type="button" class="blacksmith-tool-btn is-icon" data-tooltip="Edit"><i class="fa-solid fa-pen"></i></button></span></div><div class="blacksmith-tool-card-body">Body text</div></div>`. Expected: closed at first with a right-pointing caret; clicking the name or caret opens it and the caret turns down; Space on the focused header does the same; clicking the pen button does NOT open or close it.
13. Chip. Add `<div class="blacksmith-tool-chips"><span class="blacksmith-tool-chip">Plain</span><label class="blacksmith-tool-chip is-selectable"><input type="checkbox"><span>Pick</span></label><span class="blacksmith-tool-chip">Remove<button type="button" class="blacksmith-tool-chip-remove" data-tooltip="Remove"><i class="fa-solid fa-xmark"></i></button></span></div>`. Expected: three pills; clicking Pick tints it orange and clicking again clears it; the remove button highlights on hover; all readable on each theme.
14. Editable card title. Replace the card's title span with `<span class="blacksmith-tool-card-title"><input type="text" class="blacksmith-input" value="Group name"></span>`. Expected: you can click into the field and type, the card does not open or close while you do, and clicking the caret or the empty part of the header still toggles it.
15. Card notice. Add `<div class="blacksmith-tool-card-notice is-warning"><i class="fa-solid fa-triangle-exclamation"></i> A pin is missing.</div>` between the head and the body of a closed card. Expected: the notice shows with the card closed and stays when it opens, with an amber left edge and icon on each theme.
16. Editable text. Add `<span class="blacksmith-tool-editable" data-value="Group A"><span class="blacksmith-tool-editable-text">Group A</span><button type="button" class="blacksmith-tool-btn is-icon blacksmith-tool-editable-edit" data-tooltip="Rename"><i class="fa-solid fa-pen"></i></button></span>` to a Tool window body, and in the console `document.addEventListener('change', (e) => e.target.classList?.contains('blacksmith-tool-editable') && console.log(e.detail))`. Expected: plain text with a quiet pencil; the pencil brightens on hover; pressing it turns the text into a field with the text selected; Enter, clicking away, and Tab each put the text back and log one `{ value, previous }` when it changed; Escape puts the old text back and logs nothing.
17. Editable multiline and required. Add `data-multiline="true"` and then `data-required="true"`. Expected: Enter makes a new line and Ctrl+Enter accepts; clearing a required one and accepting keeps the old value.
18. Editable in a card title and a list row. Put the same markup inside `.blacksmith-tool-card-title` and inside `.blacksmith-list-row-title`. Expected: the pencil and the field take their own clicks and never open or close the card; the rest of the title still toggles it.
19. Redraw guard. While a field is open, run `game.modules.get('coffee-pub-blacksmith').api.toolEditable.isEditing()`. Expected: true; false once it closes.
20. Stack. Put three toggle rows in `<div class="blacksmith-tool-stack">`. Expected: one per line with a small gap, labels not touching the next switch.
21. Compact toggle. Put three `.blacksmith-toggle-row` toggles in a `.blacksmith-tool-stack` in a Tool window. Expected: a 30 by 16 switch, the label in normal case at a normal size, level with the 24px buttons; the same markup in a standard window is still 46 by 24 with an uppercase label.
22. Multi-select segmented. Use `<div class="blacksmith-tool-segmented" role="group"><label class="blacksmith-tool-segment"><input type="checkbox" checked><span>Linked</span></label><label class="blacksmith-tool-segment"><input type="checkbox"><span>Riders</span></label></div>`. Expected: each segment fills orange when ticked, independently; Tab moves between them and Space toggles.
23. Underline tabs. Use the markup in the comment above `.blacksmith-tool-tabs` with a count on one tab. Expected: normal-case labels, the open tab with an orange underline and bold text, inactive tabs readable and not faint, a hover fill, and the count pill the same height as the label's text on every theme.
24. Boxed tabs in a Tool window. Expected: inactive `.blacksmith-tab` buttons are readable dark text on Light, the active one is orange on a tint, and a `.blacksmith-tab-count` pill fits the label.
25. Joined button group. Put four `.blacksmith-tool-btn` inside `.blacksmith-tool-btn-group`. Expected: one bordered row with thin dividers, each button still hovering and disabling on its own.
26. Drop zone. Add `<div class="blacksmith-tool-dropzone"><i class="fa-solid fa-arrow-down-to-bracket"></i><span class="blacksmith-tool-dropzone-hint">Drop a pin here</span></div>` to a Tool window, then in the console add and remove `is-over` on it. Expected: a dashed border with muted hint text; `is-over` turns it solid orange on a tint; `is-filled` makes the border solid but calm; readable on every theme.
27. The four older components on the Light Tool theme. Put a `.blacksmith-tag` row, a `.blacksmith-icon-action` button, a `.blacksmith-window-section` with a header and a header action, and a `.blacksmith-panel-card` in a Tool window. Expected: no dark slab on parchment, tag and header text readable, the icon action visible, on Light, Glass and dark.
28. The same four in a standard window. Open Manage Pins (tags, icon actions) and an ordinary window using sections. Expected: unchanged from before.
29. Range slider. Put `<div class="blacksmith-slider"><i class="fa-solid fa-volume-low"></i><input type="range" min="0" max="100" value="50"><span>50%</span></div>` in a Tool window. Expected: the value readout is readable text, the track has a visible edge, and the thumb is a clear round handle, on Light, Glass and dark; the same markup in a standard window is unchanged.
30. Toggle on state. In a Tool window on Light, Glass and dark, switch a toggle on. Expected: the track is filled orange with a white thumb, clearly different from the off state; in a standard window it is unchanged.
31. Controls in a plain dialog. Put a segmented control and a `.blacksmith-tool-btn` in a DialogV2 with Foundry on its light theme, then its dark theme. Expected: unselected segment text and button text are readable, the container and borders are visible grey, the selected segment is orange with white text.
32. One lit look. In a Tool window on Light, Glass and dark, put a selected segment, a toggle that is on, a `.blacksmith-tool-btn.is-active`, a checked selectable chip, an open tab with a count, and a `.blacksmith-badge-accent` side by side. Expected: all are the same solid orange with light text or icon, and the lit button that is also disabled is not dimmed.
